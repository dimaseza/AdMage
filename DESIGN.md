# 🎨 Design System

## 1. Brand Identity
- **Name:** AdMage
- **Tone:** Profesional, modern, trustable
- **Target Audience:** Affiliators, Brands, Agencies

## 2. Color Palette (Proposed)
- **Primary:** TBD (e.g., Electric Blue or Vibrant Violet for AI feel)
- **Background:** Sleek Dark Mode (e.g., #0B0F19) with glassmorphism elements.
- **Text:** High contrast white/off-white (#F3F4F6) for readability.
- **Status Colors:**
  - Success: #10B981 (Green)
  - Warning: #F59E0B (Amber)
  - Error: #EF4444 (Red)
  - Info: #3B82F6 (Blue)

## 3. Typography
- **Primary Font:** Modern Sans-serif (e.g., `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, or `Outfit`).
- **Hierarchy:** Clear distinction between H1 (Page Titles), H2 (Section Headers), and Body text.

## 4. Components
- **Buttons:** Pill-shaped or rounded corners, distinct hover/active micro-animations (scale up, glow).
- **Cards:** Glassmorphism (translucent background, subtle borders) or soft shadows for displaying generated product/campaign images.
- **Inputs:** Accessible with clear focus rings and error states.

## 5. UI/UX Principles
- **Premium Feel:** Emphasize high-quality AI outputs with a clean, uncluttered interface.
- **Dynamic Interaction:** Smooth page transitions, hover effects on gallery items.
- **Clarity:** Transparent credit usage display and clear pricing tier differentiation.
